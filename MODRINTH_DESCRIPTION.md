# Koniavacraft

You drop coal into a generator. It starts burning. Mana flows into the network.
That's the loop — and from there you build out.

Koniavacraft is a mana-industrial mod. The idea is simple: mana is a resource
you generate, move, and spend, just like power in any tech mod. Except it's magic.

Still in beta. Actively developed. Rough around some edges.

---

## Machines

**Mana Generator**
Burns fuel to produce mana or RF energy — switchable mid-game. Coal works out of
the box. Upgrade slots for faster burn time and better output.

**Arcane Conduit**
Connects your machines into a shared mana network. Three tiers: Basic (256),
Advanced (1024), Elite (4096). Mix them freely — the pool capacity stacks.
Pull conduits out and the network shrinks. Add them back and it grows.

**Mana Grinder**
Grinds items into other items using mana. Stone becomes sand. Diamond becomes
Mana Dust, with a chance to also drop gravel. Supports two-input recipes.

**Mana Infuser**
Infuses mana into items to transform them. Cobblestone → Stone Bricks.
Iron → Mana Ingot. Apple → Golden Apple. Sand → Glass. Book → Enchanted Book.
The recipe list will grow.

**Mana Crafting Table**
Standard 3×3 grid. Costs mana per craft instead of XP or other materials.
Required for a few mod-specific recipes.

**Solar Mana Collector**
Passive mana generation during daylight. Low output, zero maintenance.
Good for topping off a network that's mostly full.

## Materials

Mana Dust → Condensed Mana Dust → Refined Mana Dust.
Corrupted Mana Dust if something goes wrong.
Mana Ingot for higher-tier crafting.

These are the currency of the mod's crafting chain.

---

## World Generation

There's a custom biome called the Mana Prairie. It generates in the overworld.
No special marker, no structure — just go explore and you'll find it.

---

## Compatibility

- NeoForge 1.21.1 only
- JEI integration included
- RF / FE energy output works with AE2 and other tech mods
